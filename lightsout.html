💡 Lights Out

How to play

  • Goal: turn every light off.
  • Click a light: it toggles that light and its neighbours directly above, below, left and right.
  • Always solvable: every board is randomly scrambled from a solved state, so a solution always exists.
  • Challenge: clear the board in as few moves as possible. The optimal count is shown as Min moves.
