🗼 Tower of Hanoi

How to play

  • Goal: move the entire stack from the left tower to the right tower.
  • Move a disk: click a tower to lift its top disk, then click another tower to drop it — or drag a disk from one tower to another.
  • Rule: you can only move one disk at a time, and you can never place a larger disk on top of a smaller one.
  • Challenge: solve it in as few moves as possible. The optimal number is shown as Min moves.
