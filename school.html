👻 Haunted Halls

How to play

  • Goal: find all 3 keys scattered through the school's halls and classrooms, then reach the exit doors to escape.
  • Look: click the view to lock the mouse, then move the mouse to look around.
  • Move: WS forward/back, ←→ turn, A/D strafe.
  • It hunts you: a shadow creature stalks the halls and takes the shortest path to you. The screen darkens red as it nears — and if it reaches you, it lunges.
  • Hide: stand by a locker and press E to climb in. It loses your trail and wanders off. Press E again to come out.
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen control pad and the E button.
