🐀 Sewer Escape

How to play

  • Goal: find all 3 keys hidden in the sewer maze and its rooms, then reach the exit gate to escape.
  • Move: WS forward/back, ←→ turn, A/D strafe. Click the view to look with the mouse.
  • Danger: a giant blood-soaked rat stalks you and takes the shortest path to your position. Don't get cornered — the screen reddens as it closes in.
  • Hide: stand next to a locker and press E to climb in. The rat loses your scent and wanders off. Press E again to come out.
  • Locked gate: the exit stays locked (red) until all 3 keys are found, then turns green.
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen control pad and the E button.
